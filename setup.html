Lens Player setup

Do this on your phone. Paste YouTube links, get one link, paste it into the Meta AI app. Done.

1Add up to 5 playlists or videos

In the YouTube app: open a playlist or video → Share → Copy link → paste here. Playlists work best (Next/Back skip videos).

2Get your glasses link

"Send to glasses" tries to open the Meta AI app's add-app screen. If nothing happens, use Copy link and step 3.
On a computer? Scan this QR with your phone's camera to add it.

3Paste it in the Meta AI app

Meta AI app → App Settings → Apps → Web Apps → Connect Web App → paste → Save. "Lens Player" appears at the bottom of the app grid in your lens.
