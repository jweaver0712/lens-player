🧭 Ride Along

Turn-by-turn directions in your glasses lens for when you're the passenger. Open this page on the phone that runs your Meta AI app.

Passenger only. Never use it while you drive. When you drive, use Apple Maps on your phone or CarPlay, and its voice plays through your glasses.

Ride time: share your location

Tap when you get in the car. Keep this page open with the screen on (it stays awake by itself). If you lock the phone or switch apps, iPhone pauses the location until you come back.

Your location goes only to your glasses. It's encrypted with a key that lives on this phone and in your glasses' Ride Along link, sent over a random private channel. Nothing is saved.

2. Favorites (optional)

Save places you go a lot, like the supply house or the shop. They show up first in the lens so you don't have to type. After you change favorites, tap the Add button above again.
