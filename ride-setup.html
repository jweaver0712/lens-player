🧭 Ride Along

Turn-by-turn directions in your glasses lens for when you're the passenger. Open this page on the phone that runs your Meta AI app.

Passenger only. Never use it while you drive. When you drive, use Apple Maps on your phone or CarPlay, and its voice plays through your glasses.
